Executive Interview Battlecards

Must-Know M&A Concepts

  • EV vs Equity Value: Enterprise Value belongs to all capital providers; Equity Value belongs to shareholders only. Bridge = Equity Value + Net Debt + Preferred + Minority Interest.
  • Accretion / Dilution Shortcut: In an all-stock deal, if the acquirer's P/E exceeds the target's P/E (post-premium), the deal is accretive to EPS.
  • Purchase Accounting: Goodwill = Purchase Equity Value − Target Book Value − Asset Write-Ups + New Deferred Tax Liabilities.

M&A Power Phrases

  • "EPS accretion is an output, not a thesis — the real question is whether the price paid is below intrinsic value plus achievable synergies."
  • "I'd triangulate across comps, precedents, and DCF, then let the football field frame the negotiating range."
  • "The cost of the acquisition currency matters: after-tax cost of debt, foregone cash yield, or the inverse of the acquirer's P/E for stock."

Metrics To Know Cold

  • Unlevered FCF: EBIT × (1 − tax rate) + D&A − CapEx − Increase in Net Working Capital.
  • WACC: (E/V) × Cost of Equity + (D/V) × Cost of Debt × (1 − tax rate).
  • Breakeven Synergies: The pre-tax synergies required to offset EPS dilution from the deal's financing cost.

Mental Math Shortcuts

  • Cost of an all-cash deal ≈ foregone after-tax interest on cash; cost of debt ≈ after-tax coupon; cost of stock ≈ E/P (inverse of acquirer P/E).
  • A 1.0x turn of EV/EBITDA on $100M of EBITDA moves headline value by $100M — anchor negotiations in turns, not percentages.
  • Rule of 72: divide 72 by the annual return to estimate years to double invested equity.

Market Levels To Track Daily

  • Rates: Fed funds target, 2-year and 10-year Treasury yields, and the shape of the curve (steep, flat, inverted).
  • Equities: S&P 500 and NASDAQ levels, VIX, and the recent IPO / follow-on issuance tone.
  • Credit: Investment-grade and high-yield index spreads, plus any large new-issue deals pricing that week.

Product Knowledge Anchors

  • ECM ladder: IPO → follow-on (marketed or bought) → block trade → convertible, each trading execution certainty against price.
  • DCM ladder: Investment-grade bonds → term loans → high-yield notes → mezzanine, each stepping down in seniority and up in coupon.
  • Greenshoe: A 15% over-allotment option that lets the syndicate stabilize the aftermarket without taking naked risk.

Capital Markets Power Phrases

  • "The issuance window matters as much as the structure — volatility closes markets faster than fundamentals reopen them."
  • "I'd frame the financing decision around rating headroom, covenant flexibility, and all-in after-tax cost, not coupon alone."
  • "Price tension in the book comes from anchoring quality accounts early and scaling allocations against aftermarket behavior."

Interview Preparation Habits

  • Read the market summary every morning of interview season; be ready to quote levels within a reasonable range, not to the decimal.
  • Follow two recent deals end-to-end — one equity issuance, one M&A transaction — and be able to discuss rationale, structure, and market reaction.
  • Prepare a one-line house view on rates and equities you can defend for two minutes without notes.
